About Free Word Counter

Welcome to Free Word Counter, a simple and free online tool for counting words and characters in your text.

What is Word Counter?

Word Counter helps you quickly check the number of words and characters in any text. It can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, teachers, and anyone who needs to check text length.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a fast, simple, and easy-to-use word counting tool that works directly in your web browser.

Free to Use

You can use our Word Counter online without installing any software.
